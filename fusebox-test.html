In-World Lore & Rules

In Dr. Kessler's stateroom on Halcyon-9, the heavy maintenance robotic arm (RoboArmAture.001) is malfunctioning and swinging violently across the room, blocking access to her research logs.

Solving this breaker puzzle disengages all 4 rotary magnetic couplings, tripping the auxiliary arm breaker, cutting power, and making the bedroom safe.

  • KEYBIND Press Space or Click/Tap to lock the active coupling.
  • TOLERANCE Lock when the rotating contact is inside the caliper bracket (±0.18 rad).
  • SPEED RAMP Rotor rotation speed increases +7% after each successful lock.
  • LIVES & TIME 3 surge capacity lives · 20.0s breaker trip countdown window.
  • END MESSAGE Shows result with single "Close" button (Enter or Esc also close it).
  • FRESH REOPEN Reopening via "Open puzzle" gives a completely fresh round.
