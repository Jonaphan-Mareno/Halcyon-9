# Flashback 3 — "The lock": Draft Script

DRAFT for proofreading. Design context: `cutscenes-flashbacks-plan.md` (sections 6 and 7). Follows Flashback 2, where Kessler says "Then I'll cut her power by hand. The others are already in the engine room."

First person through Voss's eyes, in the Level 3 engine room (shown dry and lit), about 80 s. The crew's faces are shown clearly for the first time. ARIA says nothing; she is only a light.

## Assumptions (change any you disagree with)

- In your note, "she walks out and breaks the door" is read as **Voss** walking out and breaking the door's lock mechanism **from the corridor side**. That is why the player later finds the panel in the corridor ("It's locked from out here").
- Voss has already moved ARIA to the emergency power (in another room) **before** this scene, so he is a step ahead of them. He locks them in because they would otherwise go and cut that power too.
- Voss is **cold** here, not manic as in Flashback 2: the argument is over and he has decided. His camera shows it: steady, slow breathing, hardly any sway, his gaze fixed. The cold is not meant to be unexplained; he reads as a man who has lost the argument and stopped arguing.
- The flooding is hinted only by Navarro's last line (water seeping in); its cause is never explained. His mother is not mentioned.

## The set

The engine room: a big humming machine, with a thick **main feed** coupling that runs to a conduit labelled "ARIA, PRIMARY". The door behind Voss is open. A console in the corner has ARIA's light on it. Four crew stand in front of the machine: Kessler leads, with Park holding a heavy wrench, Lindqvist and Navarro beside her.

## The scene

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Voss stands in the doorway, still. Kessler faces the crew, hands open. |
| 5–25 s | Kessler confesses and warns them. The crew react. |
| 25–35 s | They take her side. Kessler tells Park to cut the main feed. The clunk, the lights flicker, the hum dies. |
| 35–42 s | Silence. ARIA's light on the console is still pulsing. |
| 42–62 s | They turn on Voss. Kessler stares at him. He answers, coldly. |
| 62–75 s | They rush him. He steps back through the door and tears out the lock from the corridor side: sparks. They hammer on the glass and plead. |
| 75–80 s | "Stay here until I finish my research." He walks away. Behind him, as the door seals, Navarro calls out that there is water in the corner of the room. Voss does not turn or answer. Cut to black. |

## Cost and risks

- **Four speaking faces** means lip-sync for four characters in one scene, with three new face models. This is the heaviest of the three flashbacks. To keep it practical: never show more than two faces in close-up at once; keep Park, Lindqvist and Navarro mostly in mid-shots; use Kessler for the closest shots.
- **Length.** About 80 s is long for a flashback, but it is the story's central scene. Trim options: cut `fb3-navarro-1`, `fb3-park-1` and `fb3-voss-2` (about 12 s) without losing the structure.
- **First-person movement.** The cold Voss should have almost none of the sway he had in Flashback 2. When he tears out the lock, his hands are visible, so the movement has to look natural.

## Decided

- "Somewhere you won't be going" is enough: the emergency power room is never named and the player never visits it.
- Everyone except Kessler is called by surname (Kessler is "Elena" to Voss).
- The water: Navarro's last line as the door seals; Voss does not acknowledge it.
